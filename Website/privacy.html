Privacy

Two Egoes collects no data. App Store label: Data Not Collected.

  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code.
  • Your location, if you allow it, is rounded to city level and used on your device to compute the sky. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The place you choose and your language are stored on your device, and shared with the app’s own widget on the same device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
