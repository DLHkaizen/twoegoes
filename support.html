Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. Anything else: write to us.

Why does Two Egoes ask for my location?
To draw the sky exactly as it looks from where you are. It asks once, When In Use, and is content with city-level precision. If you say no, choose a place on the globe or from the list of countries — the app works the same.
How accurate is the sky?
Positions come from Jean Meeus’s Astronomical Algorithms and are checked against the U.S. Naval Observatory: moon phases within 2 minutes, moonrise and moonset within a minute, every eclipse of 2024–2028 found and classified. Distances and sizes are compressed so the system fits a screen; True scale shows them as they are.
How do I restore Two Egoes Complete?
Settings → Two Egoes Complete → Restore purchase. Complete is shared with your Family Sharing group.
Why are Ramadan and Eid marked “expected”?
The app uses the Umm al-Qura calendar. In practice these months begin when the new crescent is sighted, which differs from country to country.
